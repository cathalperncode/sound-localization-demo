Listening activity

Where did the sound seem to be?

In this task, you will hear pairs of brief noise bursts through your headphones. For each pair, you will indicate where the second sound seems to be coming from and then rate how certain you are about that location.

Before you begin

Headphone check

1. Put on both earpieces

Use a quiet place. Check the L and R markings. Turn off mono audio, spatial audio, virtual surround, and head tracking.

2. Find a comfortable volume

Start with your device volume low. Play the sound and adjust until it is clearly audible and comfortable. Keep the volume fixed during the activity.

Your task

Listen, then mark the location

On each trial, you will hear two brief sounds.

After the second sound, click the point on the arc that best matches where that sound seemed to come from.

Then indicate how certain you are about the location you selected. Choosing a certainty rating advances to the next trial. If you are unsure, give your best estimate.

The arc runs from left to right in front of the pictured head. You can also use the left and right arrow keys to select a position. The sounds may seem to be inside your head; choose the point that best matches their left–right position.
